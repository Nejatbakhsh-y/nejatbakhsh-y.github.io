## Required File Names

```text

```


## Text Replacements in index.html

Search for and replace:

```text
YOUR_PROFESSIONAL_EMAIL
YOUR_LINKEDIN_URL
YOUR_GITHUB_URL
```

Replace all placeholder biography, education, publication, project, teaching,
and professional-experience content.

## Privacy Controls

Do not publish:

- Home address
- Personal telephone number
- Social Security number
- Driver's license or passport
- Private employment documents
- Student information
- Confidential research information
- Sensitive financial or medical information
